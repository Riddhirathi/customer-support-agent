# KYC and Account Updates Policy

This document covers how customers can update their registered address, mobile number, and other
KYC details on their IIFL loan account, what documents are accepted, and the timelines involved.

## Address Change

Customers can update their registered address by submitting a request through the IIFL mobile app,
website customer portal, or at any branch. Accepted address proof documents are: Aadhaar card,
passport, voter ID, driving licence, or a recent utility bill (not older than 2 months). Address
updates submitted through the app are processed within 5 working days; branch requests are processed
within 3 working days since they are verified on the spot.

## Mobile Number Change

To change the registered mobile number, the customer must visit a branch in person with original
identity proof, since this update requires an OTP-based verification step that cannot be completed
remotely for security reasons. Mobile number changes are processed within 2 working days of the
branch visit, and a confirmation SMS is sent to both the old and new numbers. Requests to change
the mobile number without an in-person branch visit are not accepted, to prevent account takeover
fraud.

## Email Update

Email address updates can be made instantly through the app or customer portal after OTP verification
sent to the registered mobile number. No physical documents are required for email-only updates.

## Documents Accepted for KYC Re-verification

- Identity proof: PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport, voter ID, or driving licence.
- Address proof: Aadhaar card, passport, voter ID, driving licence, ration card, or utility bill
  (electricity, water, or piped gas, dated within the last 2 months).
- Income proof (for credit limit review only): latest 3 months' salary slips or latest 2 years'
  income tax returns for self-employed customers.

## Periodic KYC Re-verification

As per RBI's periodic KYC guidelines, IIFL requires customers to re-verify their KYC documents every
2 years for high-risk accounts and every 8 years for low-risk accounts. Customers are notified by
SMS and email at least 30 days before their re-verification is due. Failure to complete
re-verification within 60 days of the notice may result in temporary restrictions on further
disbursements, though existing EMI schedules are not affected.

## How to Submit a Request

1. Log in to the IIFL app or portal and navigate to "Profile" > "Update KYC Details."
2. Upload a clear scan or photo of the relevant document (file size under 5 MB, PDF or JPEG).
3. For mobile number changes, book a branch visit slot through the app instead.
4. Track the status of any update request under "My Requests"; approved changes reflect on the
   account within the timelines listed above.

Any request that involves a name change (due to marriage or legal correction) requires a gazette
notification or marriage certificate and is reviewed manually, since this cannot be verified through
the standard document checklist and should be routed to a human agent.
